## Pending applications

**AntiHunter DIGI detection node** — assembled units, populated boards, and this firmware.

U.S. provisional patent application **64/151,838**, filed 10 September 2026.
"Transceiver arbitration in a wireless detection node."

## What is claimed

The pending application is directed to:

- Arbitration of a single radio transceiver between a detection function and a triggered
  investigative response, where dissimilar triggering events are reduced to one internal
  command representation, a response is armed in a register, execution is deferred until
  the transceiver is free, one response runs per interval of availability, and an armed
  response is abandoned rather than acted upon once its triggering intelligence is stale.
- Visitation of a radio channel by scan request bearing a home-channel dwell time, so the
  transceiver leaves the channel of an access point service it operates and returns
  without loss of the associated station.

Not every feature of AntiHunter is the subject of the pending application. Nothing here
should be read as a claim to subject matter that is not.
