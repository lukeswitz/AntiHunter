                                HELLO, OPERATOR
   Thank you for joining the project. AntiHunter is fully open source: write
   your own mesh commands and connect your own IDS.
   The WIKI walks you through assembly and setup step by step. Every link is
   under RESOURCES at the bottom.
================================================================================
                                 [01]  ANTENNAS
   Never power the node with the LoRa antenna missing. It can destroy the radio.
      2.4 GHz film antenna or short duck  ->  XIAO ESP32-S3
      LoRa coiled whip or long duck       ->  Heltec / T114
      GPS ceramic patch or active helix   ->  GPS module
   U.FL: hold the connector parallel to the board with the rims aligned,
         then press STRAIGHT DOWN until it clicks. Never press at an angle
         or force it.
   SMA:  finger tight plus an eighth of a turn.
================================================================================
                                [02]  MESH RADIO
   Soldered Core and Assembled radios arrive flashed and serial-configured.
   On Bare PCB and Parts Kit, flash stable Meshtastic at flasher.meshtastic.org,
   then set SERIAL Enabled, TEXTMSG, 115200,
   pins 19RX/20TX (V3) or 10RX/9TX (T114).
   On every radio, before you deploy:
      REGION       arrives UNSET. The radio receives but does not transmit
                   until you set it.
      PAIRING PIN  is the same on every unit. Change it.
      CHANNEL      is the public default. Make your own encrypted
                   channel primary.
================================================================================
                             [03]  FLASH ANTIHUNTER
   No tier ships with AntiHunter installed. You flash it yourself, so you know
   what is on it.
      FULL      = Wi-Fi AP and web UI, plus mesh.
      HEADLESS  = serial and mesh, no AP.
   USB CABLE: a thick USB-C plug does not fit the discontinued case design.
   Use a cable with a thin plug.
   FIRST BOOT: join the access point "Antihunter" (password antihunt3r123)
   and open 192.168.4.1. Before anything else, change both under
   System > Settings > Access point. The defaults are public.
================================================================================
                                  !  POWER  !
   Use regulated 5V only, from USB or the UPS board. Unregulated input kills
   parts. Connect one USB cable at a time, because the ESP32 and the radio
   share power rails. We recommend a 2A fast-blow square TE5 fuse
   (Littelfuse 391 series) on the battery line. It is not supplied.
