--[ 1 - Introduction

Thanks for joining the project. AntiHunter is fully open source, so you
can write your own mesh commands and plug in your own IDS.

The wiki walks you through assembly and setup step by step. The sections
below are the parts you must not skip. Every link is in section 7.

--[ 2 - Antennas

Never power the node with the LoRa antenna missing. It can destroy the
radio.

    2.4 GHz film antenna or short duck   ->  XIAO ESP32-S3
    LoRa coiled whip or long duck        ->  Heltec / T114
    GPS ceramic patch or active helix    ->  GPS module

U.FL: hold the connector parallel to the board with the rims aligned,
then press straight down until it clicks. Never press at an angle and
never force it.

SMA: finger tight, plus an eighth of a turn.

--[ 3 - Power

Use regulated 5V only, from USB or the UPS board. Unregulated input
kills parts.

Connect one USB cable at a time, because the ESP32 and the radio share
power rails. We recommend a 2A fast-blow fuse on the battery line. It is
not supplied.

--[ 4 - Mesh radio

On the Soldered Core and Assembled tiers, the radio arrives flashed and
its serial link is already set.

On the Bare PCB and Parts Kit tiers, flash stable Meshtastic with the
Meshtastic web flasher, then set the serial module:

                        Heltec V3      T114
    Serial              Enabled        Enabled
    Mode                TEXTMSG        TEXTMSG
    Baud                115200         115200
    RX / TX pins        19 / 20        10 / 9

Before you deploy any radio:

    - Region arrives UNSET. The radio receives but does not transmit
      until you set it.
    - The pairing PIN is the same on every unit. Change it.
    - The channel is the public default. Make your own encrypted
      channel the primary one.

--[ 5 - Flash AntiHunter

No tier ships with AntiHunter installed. You flash it yourself with the
AntiHunter web flasher, so you know what is on it. Pick a build:

    FULL          Wi-Fi access point and web UI, plus mesh
    HEADLESS      serial and mesh, no access point

On first boot, join the Wi-Fi network "Antihunter" (password
antihunt3r123) and open 192.168.4.1. Before anything else, change both
under System > Settings > Access point. The defaults are public.
