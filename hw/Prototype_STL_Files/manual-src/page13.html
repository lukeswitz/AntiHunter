7. 3D-PRINTED ENCLOSURE PREPARATION

⚠ Follow this order

7.1 INSTALL THREADED INSERTS (FIRST)

Use the M3×3 mm short inserts from the kit (4.6 mm wide). Longer inserts do not fit.

Battery / UPS Housing

  • 4× M3 inserts – UPS module
  • 4× M3 inserts – battery housing cover

Main PCB Housing

  • 4× M3 inserts – PCB mounting
  • 2× M3 inserts – front cover, in the two housing wall posts

Additional

  • 1× threaded insert – camera / tripod mount

Push each insert in with the iron to about 90% of its depth, then press it flush with a flat tool so it sits straight.

7.2 BOND MAIN HOUSING AND BATTERY HOUSING (SECOND)

  1. Dry-fit and verify orientation
  2. Apply glue or epoxy to mating surfaces
  3. Join housings
  4. Clamp or hold until fully cured

⚠ Permanent bond – verify alignment before gluing
