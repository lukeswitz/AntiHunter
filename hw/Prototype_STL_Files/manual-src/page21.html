11. STEP 7 – INSTALL FAN AND COVERS

Front cover and optional fan

  1. Screw the fan to the front cover with 4× M3×12 mm screws (part 4). A 10 mm fan takes M3×14 mm screws. Air comes out of the side with the label. Label facing the cover blows air out of the case (exhaust). Label facing the board pulls air in (intake).
  2. Connect the fan to the fan output JST on the PCB.
  3. Screw the front cover on: 2× M3×6 mm into the wall posts and 2× M3×6 mm into the standoffs (part 2).

Back cover (last)

  1. Check the batteries are in the right way round (step 10).
  2. Screw the back cover on with 4× M3×6 mm screws (part 2).
