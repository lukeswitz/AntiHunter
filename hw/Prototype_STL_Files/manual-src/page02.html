1. INTRODUCTION

The ANTIHUNTER DIGINODE prototype is a modular, multi-radio node intended for experimentation, logging, field deployment and further development.

It integrates:

  • WiFi & Bluetooth
  • LoRa long-range radio
  • GPS positioning
  • Real-Time Clock (RTC)
  • SD card data logging
  • Tamper / vibration sensing
  • Optional active cooling
  • Battery-backed UPS with regulated 5 V output

This manual describes the complete hardware assembly, including soldering, SD card preparation, connector installation, and 3D-printed enclosure assembly.

This guide is written for hobbyists and makers and assumes basic soldering experience.

Important notice

This device is not factory waterproof. Any resistance to moisture depends on the TPU seals and any sealing you add (section 7.3). The SMA connectors and the fan vents are not watertight. No IP rating is claimed.
