10. STEP 6 – PREPARE AND INSTALL THE UPS MODULE

Install the UPS module before you fit the covers. The back cover closes over it.

  1. Prepare the power connector (JST plug with black and red wires).
  2. Solder the power connector wires to the UPS module: red to UPS+, black to UPS-.
  3. Prepare the USB-C chassis jack (with black and red wires). Thread the wires through the case hole before you solder. The nut goes on the inside and the waterproof cover on the outside.
  4. Solder the USB-C wires: red to 5V+, black to 5V-.
