                                D I G I   N O D E
==================================================================================
                                 HELLO, OPERATOR

   Thank you for joining the project. AntiHunter is fully open source: write your
   own mesh commands and connect your own IDS.

   OPERATOR'S GUIDE -- unboxing to deployment, every detector, tuning, C2:
   github.com/lukeswitz/AntiHunter/blob/main/docs/AntiHunter-Operators-Guide.pdf
==================================================================================
                                  [01]  ANTENNAS

   Never power the node with the LoRa antenna missing. It can destroy the radio.

      2.4 GHz film antenna or short duck  ->  XIAO ESP32-S3
      LoRa coiled whip or long duck       ->  Heltec / T114
      GPS ceramic patch or active helix   ->  GPS module

   U.FL: hold the connector parallel to the board with the rims aligned, then
   press STRAIGHT DOWN until it clicks. Never press at an angle or force it.
   SMA: finger tight plus an eighth of a turn.
==================================================================================
                                 [02]  MESH RADIO

   Soldered Core and Assembled radios arrive flashed and serial-configured. On
   Bare PCB and Parts Kit, flash stable Meshtastic at flasher.meshtastic.org,
   then set SERIAL Enabled, TEXTMSG, 115200, pins 19RX/20TX (V3) or 10RX/9TX
   (T114).

   On every radio, before you deploy:
      REGION arrives UNSET. The radio receives but does not transmit until you
         set it.
      PAIRING PIN is the same on every unit. Change it.
      CHANNEL is the public default. Make your own encrypted channel primary.
==================================================================================
                              [03]  FLASH ANTIHUNTER

   No tier ships with AntiHunter installed. You flash it yourself, so you know
   what is on it.
   FULL = Wi-Fi AP and web UI, plus mesh.  HEADLESS = serial and mesh, no AP.

   WEB FLASHER:  lukeswitz.github.io/AntiHunter

   FIRST BOOT: join the access point "Antihunter" (password antihunt3r123) and
   open 192.168.4.1. Before anything else, change both under System > Settings
   > Access point. The defaults are public.
==================================================================================
                                   !  POWER  !

   Use regulated 5V only, from USB or the UPS board. Unregulated input kills
   parts. Connect one USB cable at a time, because the ESP32 and the radio share
   power rails. We recommend a 2A fast-blow fuse on the battery line. It is
   not supplied.
